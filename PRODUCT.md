# Rewa Mehta · Product & UX Design Portfolio

Rewa is a UI/UX and product designer with a background in science (B.Sc, IGU Rewari) and an M.Des in UI/UX from NIFT New Delhi. The portfolio is built specifically for design hiring teams, recruiters, and startup founders.

## Core Strategy & Project Architecture
The portfolio showcases 8 verified projects directly documented on Behance (https://www.behance.net/revamehta1):

### 1. Featured Case Study (Clinical UX)
- **Real-Time Neonatal Care Monitoring: Doctor Dashboard** (Solo, 2025):
  Centralized real-time clinical telemetry dashboard and caregiver mobile logging app. Researched NICU workflows (Dr. Ananya Sharma, Nurse Priya Mehta), 5 core parameters (HR, RR, SpO2, Feeding, Temp), and an interactive 10-screen Figma prototype designed to mitigate clinical alarm fatigue.

### 2. Selected Work Index (5 Projects)
- **Manjha AI** (Team of 4: Suraj Mourya, Soumya Choudhary, Rewa Mehta, Princy Yadav, Dec 2025):
  Conversational assistance and gamified persona builder for Indian matrimony, solving chat inertia and decision anxiety with privacy-first consent and KYC verification.
- **Leaflet** (Solo, Nov 2025, NIFT M.Des MD/24/395, Figma + Canva):
  Micro-reading habit platform featuring AI physical page scanning to comic panels, silent reading rooms ("3 around you"), and guilt-free streak rings.
- **Swipe. Match. Ghost. Scam?** (Team of 2, May 2025):
  Qualitative trust and safety UX study on Indian dating apps (Bumble 15.7% India traffic), examining gender disparity (70:30) and catfishing through 60 user responses.
- **Survival of the Fittest** (Team of 6 incl. Lisha Batra — names are on the deck's last slide, Nov 2025):
  Systems-thinking and field research investigation into Delhi's UPSC coaching ecosystem following the 2024 basement flooding, utilizing Causal Loop Modeling and proposing 3 exit-ramp solutions.
- **Greenwashed on Wheels** (Team of 5, June 2025):
  Illustrated systems critique of electric vehicle lifecycle emissions in India, examining 76% coal power grid dependence, water-intensive lithium extraction, and total cost of ownership.

### 3. Playground & Inquiry (2 Visual Artifacts)
- **Male Gaze & Gender Norms** (Solo, Oct 2025):
  Editorial illustration analyzing patriarchal surveillance and societal victim-blaming advice.
- **Redesigning Care** (Solo, May 2025, NIFT MD/24/395):
  Information design poster mapping the Indian healthcare UX market ($90B to $380B+), 7 specialized design roles, and systemic opportunity sectors.
